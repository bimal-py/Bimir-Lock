Bimir Lock is an offline password manager that keeps your passwords encrypted on your own phone. No account to create, no cloud to trust, no ads. Save every login once, open it with your PIN, fingerprint or Face ID, and fill it into the app in front of you.

YOUR PASSWORDS STAY ON YOUR PHONE
• Passwords and their notes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved
• The key is kept in Android Keystore and never leaves the device
• No sign-up, no email, no server: there is no account for anyone to break into
• Works fully offline, on a plane, abroad or with mobile data off
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking

ONLY YOU CAN OPEN THEM
• Every saved password opens only with your 6-digit PIN, or your fingerprint or face if you turn it on
• Easy PINs like 123456 or 111111 are refused
• Seven wrong PINs in a row lock the keypad: 30 seconds, then 1, 5 and 15 minutes
• Wrong PIN alerts list every lockout and can photograph whoever was guessing (optional; photos stay on your phone)
• A revealed password hides again after 30 seconds, and a copied one is cleared from the clipboard after 30 seconds

STRONG PASSWORDS, MADE FOR YOU
• Password generator from 8 to 64 characters, with numbers and symbols on or off
• Leaves out look-alike characters such as l, I, O and 0
• Strength meter on every password
• Warns you before you reuse a password you already saved

PASSWORD CHECKUP
• Finds weak passwords and passwords used on more than one site
• Shows what to fix first, with a Change button on each
• Checked on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere

AUTOFILL IN OTHER APPS
• Fill saved logins into other apps and browsers that support Android autofill
• Save a new login when you sign in somewhere, without opening Bimir Lock
• Nothing is filled until you confirm with your fingerprint, face or screen lock

BACKUP YOU CONTROL
• Back up to one encrypted file, locked with its own backup password (Argon2id + AES-256-GCM)
• Keep it wherever you like: on the phone, in Google Drive, on a computer
• Restore on a new phone: it adds what is missing and never deletes anything

CALM AND EASY TO USE
• A clean list with search and letter icons
• Site name suggestions as you type, including local banks and wallets
• The username field adapts: email, phone number, customer ID or Wi-Fi network
• Light and dark themes
• A short security quote each time you open it
• A notes space for everyday notes (keep secrets in a password's encrypted note)

WHO IT IS FOR
• Anyone who wants a password keeper without handing their passwords to a cloud service
• Anyone tired of the notebook, the notes app or the same password everywhere
• Families and small shops keeping bank, email, Wi-Fi and wallet logins in one place

WHAT IT IS NOT
There is no cloud sync and no account, so your vault lives on one phone: keep a backup file somewhere safe. Bimir Lock does not check passwords against breach lists and does not make 2FA codes.

Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Made by one developer in Pokhara, Nepal.
